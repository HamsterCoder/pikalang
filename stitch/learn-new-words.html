el café

[ehl kah-FEH] Noun • Masculine
the coffee
Example Sentence

Yo bebo un café con leche por la mañana.

"I drink a coffee with milk in the morning."

lightbulb
CULTURAL NOTE

In Spain and Latin America, "café con leche" is a morning staple, typically made with equal parts strong espresso and steamed whole milk!
